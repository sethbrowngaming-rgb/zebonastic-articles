WHAT THIS VIDEO COVERS
- Rockstar says Grand Theft Auto VI releases at midnight local time on November 19, 2026, in all regions, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
- New Zealand reaches midnight first: 6 AM Eastern / 3 AM Pacific on November 18, about 18 hours before US Eastern midnight.
- The US unlock is unclear: Rockstar's wording points to each zone's own midnight, while reported PlayStation Store listings show one Eastern time for the whole country. Check your own store's countdown.
- The "New Zealand trick" on Xbox (setting the console location to New Zealand and restarting) is reported by players to work. No confirmation from Rockstar or Microsoft was found.
- Xbox One owners can stream the game through Xbox Cloud Gaming starting 1 AM ET on November 19, using Xbox Series S graphics settings. The game is bought separately.
- Not established: whether the New Zealand trick changes the Xbox Cloud Gaming start time. No source reviewed says.
