Table 3. What we know, what we think, what we do not know

Applies to the claim "49.9% of new Steam games used AI in October 2026."

WE KNOWValve's form asks about AI content that ships to players, not efficiency tools. A community tally built on Steam's flag reports 49.9% of October 2026 releases and 40.5% of 2026 releases to Nov 24 carrying it. The flag is self-reported. Disclosing releases numbered 2,319, 4,937 and 12,351 across 2024, 2025 and 2026 to date.
WE THINKDisclosed AI use among new releases rose substantially since 2024: two separate analyses of the same flag point the same direction. Art is the most commonly named use. The rise is concentrated in games with few reviews. The figures are likely lower bounds on disclosed-category AI use, because undisclosed use is invisible.
WE DO NOT KNOWHow much of the rise is real adoption, more disclosure, a changed release mix, or the January 2026 rule change. How much AI any single game used. How accurate the analyst's release dates and use categories are, since parts of the method are undocumented. Whether AI disclosure lowers reviews or low-review games simply disclose more.
