Title: Does 49.9% of Steam Use AI? What the Number Actually Counts

49.9% of games released on Steam in October 2026 carried an AI disclosure. That is one month. For 2026 so far it is 40.5%.

The flag is self-reported. It covers AI content players see or hear, not coding tools, and it can't say how much AI a game used. Flagged releases grew from 2,319 in 2024 to 12,351 in 2026 so far, but the sources don't establish why: more AI use, more disclosure, a different release mix, or the January 2026 rule change.
