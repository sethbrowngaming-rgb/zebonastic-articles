## Fact-check outcome (11 claims checked)
- **Confirmed as-is:** Värtinen et al. Aalto GPT-2 quest study (978 quests/6 RPGs/349 players/~20% acceptance); HTN/Oblivion case (Kelly et al., AIIDE); Fortnite AI Vader jailbreak (5 independent outlets); *Where Winds Meet* ketchup/Song-dynasty anachronism (single outlet); Inworld $50M/$500M raise (dated Aug 2023).
- **Corrected:** Death by AI reached 20M players in **three** months, not two (vendor's own case study).
- **Removed — unsupported:** Death by AI's "$5,000 → $250,000" cost-spike figure (no source found anywhere, including the vendor's own detailed case study); inZOI/NVIDIA ACE "players reported flat emotional engagement" claim (no independent reception data located).

Mage Arena was reclassified as a **speech-recognition** game, not generative AI — a category boundary case, not a true AI-native quest example.

## Key takeaway for future GRW pieces
Of 11 checked claims, 2 required outright removal and 2 more required a confidence downgrade — evidence that secondary/tertiary coverage on this AI-in-games beat should be checked claim-by-claim rather than trusted at face value, even when it originates from a single detailed vendor case study.
