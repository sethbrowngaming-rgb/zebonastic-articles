# Fact-Check Pass: ASUS PG259QWS Ace article

Pass run on every sentence with numbers, dates, quotes, specs, named people, and the words because/caused/designed/first/fastest/always/never.

## Result summary
- CONFIRMED: Oct 28 availability; core specs as ASUS-stated; official-monitor designation; frame-interval and bandwidth arithmetic (recomputed in code: 16.667, 6.944, 4.167, 2.778, 1.852, 1.389 ms; 22.39, 33.59, 44.79 Gbps); TFTCentral link rates; LG study parameters as reported; Copenhagen 2024 XL2566K 360 Hz; PGL date discrepancy.
- INFERRED: full-resolution 720 Hz is the notable engineering step; DP 2.1 consistent with uncompressed 720 Hz; one-frame persistence floor applies only if the game renders at refresh rate; Position Sensor/scales target setup reproducibility (design purpose undocumented).
- SPECULATIVE (labelled as such in article): LG panel supply timing as a reason for the selection; swivel read from printed scale.
- UNSUPPORTED claims removed or rewritten: none left. Items rewritten during review: (1) 540 Hz panel type now cited to ASUS, not secondary sources; (2) swivel statement now cites Guru3D and VideoCardz; (3) HDMI figure uses TFTCentral's 42.0 Gbps rather than a remembered figure; (4) "every player will use" kept only as a single-source, unverified item; (5) reading time corrected.

## Devil's advocate findings
- Vendor-sourced specs dominate; article states this and labels tandem figures as claims.
- Secondary outlets repeat ASUS text; article notes agreement among them is weak evidence.
- Bandwidth model excludes blanking; stated on the chart and in text.
- LG study cannot support 720 Hz or CS2 or pro-player claims; article says so.
- Quotes: kept short, one per source (ASUS product page "fastest esports monitor ever built"; ASUS press release "for decades").

## Not verified
Retail price at availability, regional dates, sensor accuracy, burn-in behavior, subpixel layout, sustained SDR brightness.
