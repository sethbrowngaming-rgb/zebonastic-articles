TABLE 3 — WHAT WE KNOW, THINK, AND DON'T KNOW

Status as of Nov 22, 2026, based on sources the author could retrieve.

StatusStatement
WHAT WE KNOWIn March, Nvidia and several outlets described developer intensity and color-grading controls. Hogwarts Legacy shipped DLSS 5 on Nov 5, and Avalanche says it tuned environment lighting, tone and skin structural parameters. Nvidia's current documentation says faces can be masked out. Independent tests of the September model on an RTX 5090 at 4K measured 51–60% frame-rate loss.
WHAT WE THINKNvidia's promotional Hogwarts comparisons concentrate on environmental lighting and materials, and faces look essentially unchanged in them. The difference from March may reflect settings chosen for this game, a retrained model, or both; the sources do not separate them. Cost on the shipped build looks lower than in the September NBA 2K27 tests, but the data cannot separate model improvement from game and scene differences.
WHAT WE DON'T KNOWWhether the shipped build changes characters in motion or in every scene. Why Avalanche chose its settings. Whether the claimed 2x speedup holds below the RTX 5090. Whether players prefer the result.
