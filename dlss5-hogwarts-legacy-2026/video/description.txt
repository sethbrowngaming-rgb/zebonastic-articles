Title: DLSS 5 in Hogwarts Legacy: What the Evidence Does and Doesn't Show

In March, critics said Nvidia's DLSS 5 rewrote character faces. On November 5, Hogwarts Legacy shipped it, and Nvidia's own comparison images show a gentler presentation.

Does that settle the artistic-intent question? The sources document the difference, not the reason. It could be developer settings, a retrained model, or both. Independent performance tests so far are limited, and the numbers in this video come from different games and scenes, so they are not a like-for-like comparison.
