Take-Two says GTA VI made $3.79 billion on day one. A tracker says $1.84 billion at launch. Which is right? Both may be, because they count different things.

Take-Two's release never defines "retail sales" and gives no day-one unit count. Sensor Tower's tracker estimates PlayStation and Xbox digital sales only. Divide Take-Two's own dollars by its own units and you get $116.6 per game, above the $99.99 top list price. The sources document the gap but do not establish the reason.
