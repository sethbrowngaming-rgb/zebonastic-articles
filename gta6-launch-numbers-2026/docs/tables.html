Table 3. What we know, what we think, what we do not know

Applies to the claim "GTA VI sold $3.79 billion on day one."

WE KNOWTake-Two and Rockstar said, in a press release furnished to the SEC on Nov 20, 2026, that GTA VI day one retail sales reached $3.79 billion and that 32.5 million units sold pre-release. The release calls the first a company estimate through end of day Nov 19 and does not define "retail sales." Sensor Tower's PlayStation and Xbox estimates are lower, between $1.84B and $2.36B.
WE THINKThe two sets of numbers probably measure different scopes and cut-offs, not rival versions of one truth. Sensor Tower's own units and dollars are internally consistent with list prices; Take-Two's pair is not, at $116.6 per unit, which points to a scope or timing difference inside Take-Two's own statement. The launch is very large on any of these counts.
WE DO NOT KNOWWhat "retail sales" includes. How much of $3.79B came from launch-day purchases versus earlier orders. Day-one units. How the 2013 and 2026 "day one" windows compare. What Take-Two's audited net bookings will show. The sources document the gap but do not establish the reason for it.
